Support

Found a bug, have an idea, or a recipe Chef couldn’t read? Write to matej@trexio.cz, or use Settings › Send Feedback in Chef.

Questions

Where are my recipes stored?

On your device. A copy goes to iCloud Drive only if you turn on “Back Up to iCloud”. Chef has no accounts and no server.

How do I keep them safe?

In Chef, open Settings. “Back Up All Recipes” saves a file wherever you choose, and “Back Up to iCloud” saves one to iCloud Drive automatically whenever your recipes or shopping list change.

How do I move to a new iPhone or iPad?

Restore your device backup, or in Chef open Settings › Restore from Backup and pick a Chef backup file. Recipes already in Chef aren’t duplicated; if the backup has a newer version of one, it replaces yours.

Can I bring recipes from another app?

Yes, from Recipe Keeper, My Recipe Box and RecipeChef. Export from that app, then in Chef tap + › From a File and pick the file.

Why didn’t a link import?

Some websites refuse apps, and Instagram links aren’t supported. Take screenshots of the recipe and add them with + › From Screenshots.

Do tips unlock anything?

No. Everything in Chef is free. Tips only support its development.
