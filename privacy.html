Privacy Policy

Effective 24.10.26

Your recipes stay on your device. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The only thing Chef can send us is an anonymous crash report, and only if you turn that on.

Who we are

Chef is made by SportDev s.r.o. Questions about privacy: matej@trexio.cz.

What stays on your device

Your recipes, their photos, tags, notes and your shopping list are stored only on your device. They are included in your device’s own backups (iCloud Backup or a computer), as you have set them up.

Text in screenshots, photos and scanned cookbook pages is recognized on the device. Chef doesn’t send the pictures anywhere; they leave your device only in backups you make or recipes you share. The camera is used only when you scan a page or take a photo of a dish.

When Chef uses the internet

  • Importing from a link. Chef downloads that web page, and the recipe’s photo, directly from the website. The website sees an ordinary request, including your IP address, as it would from a browser, under its own privacy policy.
  • Importing from My Recipe Box. Photos the export links to are downloaded from the recipes’ websites.
  • Opening Support Chef. The tips and their prices are loaded from Apple’s App Store.

Nothing else is sent. Apart from feedback you email us yourself, the only thing that can reach us is a crash report, described below.

iCloud backups

If you turn on “Back Up to iCloud”, Chef saves backup files in your iCloud Drive, in the Chef folder. They are stored by Apple under Apple’s Privacy Policy. We have no access to them. “Back Up to iCloud” is off until you turn it on.

Crash reports

If you turn on “Send Crash Reports” in Chef’s settings, Chef sends us a technical report when it crashes or freezes, and timings of tasks such as importing a link or making a backup as you use it. A report contains where in Chef’s code the problem happened, Chef’s version, the iOS version, and technical details of the device such as its model, language, time zone, memory and screen size. It contains nothing that identifies you or your device, so a report can’t be traced back to you.

A report never contains your recipes, photos, links, shopping list, names, device name or IP address. We use reports only to fix problems in Chef. They are stored on our own server, run by SportDev s.r.o., and deleted after 90 days. No one else receives them. Reports are off until you turn them on, and you can turn them off at any time in Chef’s settings.

Feedback

If you send feedback, it is an ordinary email from your mail account. We receive your email address, your message, and the details Chef fills in: Chef’s version, the iOS version and the device model, and the error if Chef couldn’t open your recipes. You can delete them before sending. We use them only to reply and to fix problems, and delete them when they’re no longer needed.

Tips

Tips are in-app purchases handled by Apple. We don’t receive your name, payment details or any other personal information, only Apple’s sales totals.

Children

The app itself collects no personal data from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with its date.
