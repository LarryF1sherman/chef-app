Privacy Policy

Effective 19.11.26

Your recipes are private. Chef keeps them on your devices. If iCloud is on for Chef, it also syncs them through your private iCloud. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The only thing Chef can send us is an anonymous crash report, and only if you turn that on.

Who we are

Chef is made by SportDev s.r.o., Na Folimance 2155/15, 120 00 Praha 2, Czech Republic, company ID (IČO) 10665081. It is the controller of the data described here. Questions about privacy: matej@trexio.cz.

Where your recipes are stored

Your recipes, their photos, tags and notes, your week plan and your shopping list are stored on your devices and, when iCloud is on for Chef, in your private iCloud database (below). They are included in your device’s own backups (iCloud Backup or a computer), as you have set them up.

Text in screenshots, photos and scanned cookbook pages is recognized on the device. Chef doesn’t send the pictures anywhere; they leave your device only in backups you make or recipes you share. The camera is used only when you scan a page or take a photo of a dish.

When Chef uses the internet

  • Importing from a link. Chef downloads that web page, and the recipe’s photo, directly from the website. The website sees an ordinary request, including your IP address, as it would from a browser, under its own privacy policy.
  • Syncing through iCloud. If iCloud is on for Chef, Chef sends your recipes, week plan and shopping list to your private iCloud database (below).
  • Importing from My Recipe Box. Photos the export links to are downloaded from the recipes’ websites.
  • Opening Support Chef. The tips and their prices are loaded from Apple’s App Store.

Nothing else is sent. Apart from feedback you email us yourself, the only thing that can reach us is a crash report, described below.

iCloud sync

If you’re signed in to iCloud and iCloud is on for Chef, Chef syncs your recipes, their photos, tags and notes, your week plan and your shopping list through your private iCloud database, so they’re the same on all your devices signed in to your Apple Account. Apple stores them under Apple’s Privacy Policy. We have no access to them. To stop syncing, turn off iCloud for Chef in your device’s Settings; your recipes stay on the device.

A recipe you delete stays in Recently Deleted on all your devices for 30 days, then Chef deletes it for good. Backup files that earlier versions of Chef saved to your iCloud Drive stay there until you delete them.

Crash reports

If you turn on “Send Crash Reports” in Chef’s settings, Chef sends us a technical report when it crashes or freezes, and timings of tasks such as importing a link or making a backup as you use it. A report contains where in Chef’s code the problem happened, Chef’s version, the iOS version, and technical details of the device such as its model, language, time zone, memory and screen size. It contains nothing that identifies you or your device, so a report can’t be traced back to you.

A report never contains your recipes, photos, links, shopping list, names, device name or IP address. We use reports only to fix problems in Chef. They are stored on our own server, run by SportDev s.r.o., and deleted after 90 days. No one else receives them. Reports are off until you turn them on, and you can turn them off at any time in Chef’s settings.

Feedback

If you send feedback, it is an ordinary email from your mail account. We receive your email address, your message, and the details Chef fills in: Chef’s version, the iOS version and the device model, and the error if Chef couldn’t open your recipes. You can delete them before sending. We use them only to reply and to fix problems, and delete them when they’re no longer needed.

Tips

Tips are in-app purchases handled by Apple. We don’t receive your name, payment details or any other personal information, only Apple’s sales totals.

Children

The app itself collects no personal data from anyone, children included.

Legal basis and your rights

Crash reports are sent on the basis of your consent, which you give by turning them on and withdraw by turning them off. Feedback emails are processed so we can answer you and fix what you report (our legitimate interest).

You have the right to access, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to withdraw your consent. Write to matej@trexio.cz. Crash reports contain nothing that identifies you, so we can’t find the ones that came from your device. You can also complain to the Czech data protection authority, the Office for Personal Data Protection (ÚOOÚ).
